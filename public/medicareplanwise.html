Coverage options

Understand every type of Medicare plan

There's no one-size-fits-all answer. I'll help you weigh each option against your health needs, budget, and the doctors you love.

Part C

Medicare Advantage

All-in-one coverage that often bundles medical, hospital, and prescription drugs — sometimes with extras like dental and vision.

  • Bundled benefits
  • Often $0 premium options
  • Network-based care
Medigap

Medicare Supplement

Pairs with Original Medicare to help cover out-of-pocket costs like copays, coinsurance, and deductibles.

  • See any doctor that accepts Medicare
  • Predictable costs
  • No referrals needed
Part D

Prescription Drug Plans

Stand-alone coverage that helps lower the cost of the medications you take — matched to your specific prescriptions.

  • Personalized drug review
  • Pharmacy comparisons
  • Annual cost checkups
Supplemental

Ancillary Products

Round out your coverage with policies that help with the costs Medicare doesn't — so an unexpected diagnosis or hospital stay doesn't derail your budget.

  • Cancer, heart attack & stroke
  • Hospital indemnity
  • Dental, vision & hearing

Questions

Answers to what members ask most

Does it cost anything to work with Medicare PlanWise?

No. My services are completely free to you. I'm compensated by the insurance carriers when you enroll, and that compensation is the same regardless of which plan you choose — so my advice stays unbiased.

Are you connected to one specific insurance company?

No. Medicare PlanWise is an independent advisory practice, and I work with all major national and regional carriers. That independence lets me recommend what's genuinely best for you, not what's best for a single company.

When can I make changes to my Medicare plan?

Most people can make changes during the Annual Enrollment Period (October 15 – December 7), as well as during certain special enrollment periods. I'll help you understand which windows apply to you.

What should I have ready for my consultation?

It helps to have a list of your current doctors, your prescriptions and dosages, and your Medicare card if you already have one. Don't worry if you're missing something — I'll guide you through it.

Who will I be working with?

You'll work directly with me from start to finish. I know your situation personally and am available year-round for questions, claims help, and annual plan reviews.
